# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Focus criticism on code, evidence,
and documented behavior. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and
publication of another person's credentials or private data are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact method on the
maintainer's verified GitHub profile. The maintainer may edit or remove
content, lock discussions, or temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when needed to protect contributors and users.

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, release
channels, and other project spaces.
